Why customer figures don't tie to the revenue totals above.
Revenue by Account and Revenue by Month come from the QuickBooks P&L, which is verified against the general ledger. Customer-level detail isn't available at that same level of accuracy — QuickBooks' Sales by Customer report is invoice-based, includes a large "Not specified" bucket for line items with no customer tag, and doesn't tie exactly to booked revenue. "% Collected" pulls from a third report, A/R Aging Summary, which tracks open invoice balances rather than the same revenue figures — that's why a client's revenue less its outstanding balance won't always match its collected percentage to the penny. Treat all client-level figures as directional (who's biggest, what's collected, what's outstanding), not as exact revenue or GL balances.
