How to read it. A value of 250 means ₹100 became ₹250. "vs Nifty" is how far the stock is ahead or behind the index in percent: +20% means it is worth 20% more than the same money in the Nifty. In the "Relative to Nifty" view, the index is the flat line at 100, so anything above it is beating the Nifty.

Late listings. Eternal (Jul 2021), Jio Financial (Aug 2023) and Max Healthcare (Aug 2020) did not trade on 1 Jan 2020. Their lines start on their first trading day at the Nifty's value that day, so the comparison stays fair.

Data. Yahoo Finance adjusted closes (dividends included in stocks, not in the Nifty price index), 1 Jan 2020 to 25 Sep 2026. Two non-economic jumps are removed: the Tata Motors demerger on 14 Oct 2025 (TMPV) and a data break in Trent on 1 Jan 2026. This is today's Nifty 50 list, so stocks that were in the index earlier and left are not shown.
