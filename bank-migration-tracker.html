How the Bank 2 check works. A name counts as seen at Bank 2 when NetSuite shows a payment, bill payment or check for that customer or Customer 163 on the Bank 2 account. NetSuite data runs through Sep 30. Amounts and dates come from the Bank 1 files through Oct 12. Bank 2 bank credits from Oct 1 to 14 do not carry payer names, so someone who started paying Bank 2 this month can still show as "No Bank 2 activity" until those deposits are booked. Related-party wires (Company 1, Company 2 entities) and internal transfers are left out. Customer lockbox payers appear individually where NetSuite named them.
