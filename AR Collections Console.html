Methodology. Open invoice balances (status = open) were pulled live from NetSuite. Aging tier is driven by each account's single oldest open invoice, not a blended average — one very old invoice is enough to escalate the whole account.

Escalation ladder. Tier 1 (1–30 days): listed for visibility only — mostly payment float, and no notice is drafted automatically. Tier 2 (31–60): friendly reminder. Tier 3 (61–90): firm reminder + AR follow-up call. Tier 4 (91–120): urgent notice, elevated to CFO review, and a logged phone call is required before any further notice. Tier 5 (120+): final notice, evaluate credit hold, refer to collections/legal.

Response tracking & auto-escalation. Click any row to log a customer response. No response leaves the tier purely aging-driven. Promise to pay pauses escalation until the promised date — if that date passes with no "Paid in full" logged, the account is auto-flagged "Broken promise" and bumped one tier up. Payment plan requested pauses escalation while active. Pending Action — "Customer responded, looking into it" or "Customer requested support" — groups accounts where the customer has engaged but hasn't committed to a date; escalation is paused and the account is called out in its own bucket (separate from Tier 1–4) until the status is updated. Disputed/short-paid is pulled out of the normal ladder into its own bucket until resolved. Paid in full drops the account off the active list. Escalate to legal/collections forces the account to top priority until manually closed out.

Shared & editable. This dashboard saves itself — anyone with edit access can log a response and it updates for everyone. Grant teammates access via this artifact's own Share button.
